# Visual system
Experience mode. A moss-green leather-like volume on a quiet flax-colored surface. Faded gold tooling, warm ivory paper, brown-black ink. The cover is purely typographic: Times New Roman, uppercase SELECTED POEMS, the retained fleuron, and SARA KIM at the bottom. No cover image, image texture, subtitle, footer, or opening button.

IM Fell English preserves irregular historical printed letterforms inside. Poem headings are slightly larger and italic. Stanzas, line breaks, deliberate internal spaces, italics and indentation retain the published source. Pagination measures real typeset content at the current viewport and moves whole stanzas where possible. A larger book uses available height and width; phones retain readable single leaves. A plain reading view is always available.

Artwork is tied to poem imagery: stars and cosmic alignment, hanging photographs, original sunset and moon photographs, a drawn moonlit sea. The earlier garden assets remain historical explorations and are not used. Do not place generic illustrations against unrelated poems. The full sea spread follows I Wandered Through the Sea.

Central binding shadows and page edges establish depth. One signature motion: user-driven page folding while the closed volume recenters as it opens. Keyboard navigation offers an alternative. Reduced motion removes recentering transitions and uses immediate programmatic turns.
