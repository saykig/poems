# Visual system
Experience mode. A moss-green leather-like volume on a quiet flax-colored surface. Faded gold tooling, framed garden illustration, warm ivory paper, brown-black ink. IM Fell English provides irregular historical printed letterforms; italic titles remain only slightly larger than poem text. Drawing-led vintage children's illustration grows around whitespace. Full-spread art shares one continuous image across both leaves. Central binding shadows and paper edges establish physical depth. One signature motion: user-driven page folding with the closed volume recentering as it opens. Desktop presents two pages; narrow screens present a readable single page. Small unobtrusive navigation, keyboard alternatives and a plain reading view accompany dragging. Reduced motion uses immediate page changes. No invented Sara poems.
