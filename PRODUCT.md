# Selected Poems
Sara Kim's poetry collection, experienced as a physical book. Readers arrive at a closed book and drag the pages. Two-page spreads and a central binding are essential; phones use one page at a time. Existing free page-turning components are reused.

Seven poems are selected from the ten imported verbatim from https://sarakim23.vercel.app/writing/poetry on 2026-10-19. See-through, Passage of Time, and There is Grace in the Unknown were removed from the public collection in November 2026. Preserve titles, wording, capitalization, stanza and line breaks, italics, and deliberate indentation. Do not silently correct spelling. All content is Sara's published writing, not placeholder material. This is art/site work; do not load the academic prose profile.

Confirmed cover: SELECTED POEMS, Times New Roman style, retained fleuron, SARA KIM at bottom, no images or extra subtitle. Book is the large centerpiece; no footer, opening slogan or open-book button. Only the drag hint belongs beneath the book. Interior poem pages contain text only; earlier artwork is archived outside the published site.
