Sara Kim's poetry collection, experienced as a physical illustrated storybook. Readers arrive at a closed book and drag the pages. Two-page spreads and a central binding are essential; phones use one page at a time. Existing free page-turning components are reused.

Ten poems are imported verbatim from https://sarakim23.vercel.app/writing/poetry in content/poems.json (2026-10-19). Preserve titles, wording, capitalization, stanza and line breaks, italics, and deliberate indentation. Do not silently correct spelling. All content is Sara's published writing, not placeholder material. This is art/site work; do not load the academic prose profile.

Confirmed cover: SELECTED POEMS, Times New Roman style, retained fleuron, SARA KIM at bottom, no images or extra subtitle. Book is the large centerpiece; no footer, opening slogan or open-book button. Only the drag hint belongs beneath the book. Interior artwork must relate to the actual poem; preserve the source photographs associated with I miss and The Space Between Us.
